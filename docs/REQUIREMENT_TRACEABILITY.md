# Case-Challenge Requirement Traceability

## Scope

This is a **design-coverage matrix**, not a live campaign-success sign-off. “DESIGN COVERED” means the strategy or prototype addresses the requirement; it does not establish recruited students, partner agreements, sent reminders, live AI agents, revenue, or eight successful weekly outcomes.

The [public website](https://sandai-seminar-demo-qkpntto5ya-uc.a.run.app/) is the reviewer entry point. The [strategy PDF](../strategy/Piter_Garcia_SaNDAI_8_Week_Seminar_Growth_Plan.pdf) explains the complete operating proposal.

## Demonstrated versus proposed

| Demonstrated in the prototype | Still requires real-world implementation or approval |
| --- | --- |
| Weekly and cumulative attendance calculations | Real attendance collection |
| Historical-data forecast and recovery estimate | Causal proof of intervention impact |
| Risk thresholds and recommended actions | Live operational controls and staff assignments |
| Channel contribution using synthetic data | Real partner agreements and attribution |
| Opt-in / qualified / approved-conversion structure | Approved offer, pricing, terms, and revenue |
| Guided student AI-response review | Live seminar delivery and student outcomes |

The sample contains 183 total attendances and a Week 3 miss. The miss remains visible because the requirement is weekly as well as cumulative.
