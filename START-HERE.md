# Start here

## Open the interactive website

[SaNDAI Growth & Learning Studio](https://sandai-seminar-demo-qkpntto5ya-uc.a.run.app/) is the primary browser experience. No sign-in or notebook setup is required.

- Management Command Center: https://sandai-seminar-demo-qkpntto5ya-uc.a.run.app/#management
- Student Career Lab: https://sandai-seminar-demo-qkpntto5ya-uc.a.run.app/#student

All displayed campaign results are illustrative sample data.

## A three-minute review

1. **Management:** select Week 3 to see the missed weekly target. Review the Week 4 recovery case, channel contribution, cumulative trajectory, and downstream value funnel.
2. **Student value:** open the Career Lab, choose a practice scenario, review the worked example, answer the five review questions, and build a realistic takeaway.
3. **Ownership:** review the operating cadence and the distinction between one accountable owner and collaborative execution.
4. **Strategy:** open the PDF for the complete eight-week plan, network expansion, measurement, recovery, AI support, monetization boundaries, and ownership case.

The sample totals 183 attendances but meets only seven of eight weekly targets. That is intentionally **not** presented as a successful eight-week campaign; it demonstrates how a miss remains visible and informs the next decision.
